Upload your resume and pick the role you want. We compare it with what live postings for that role ask for, then suggest projects to build that would fill the most-wanted gaps.

Your file is read once and never saved. Your email, phone number and links are removed before the text is analysed. The analysis uses Google's free Gemini service, which may use what it receives to improve its products.

How ready is your GitHub?

We read your own public repos (forks don't count) and look for proof of each skill employers ask for in your target role: the languages you write, the libraries you use, Dockerfiles and CI.
